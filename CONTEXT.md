# Monster game

A pet-raising game: the player hatches, feeds, cleans and trains monsters, then sends them to battle.

## Language

**Farm**:
The player's one shared room that shows every living monster doing what its needs call for (sleeping, eating, using the toilet). It is a picture of the monsters' state, never a source of it.
_Avoid_: Home, room, house

**Furniture**:
An item the player buys with coins and places in the Farm; each kind stretches one Care timer for all the player's monsters, even while the player is offline.
_Avoid_: Decoration, building, upgrade

**Furniture tier**:
How far a piece of Furniture is upgraded (1 to 3). Tier 1 is bought with coins; tiers 2 and 3 are crafted, always succeed, and the old tier keeps working until the new one is collected.
_Avoid_: Level, grade (grade belongs to equipment)

**Care timer**:
One of the four clocks of a monster's upkeep: how long until it is hungry, until it is weak, until it makes a poop, and until hunger makes it sick.
_Avoid_: Rate, decay
